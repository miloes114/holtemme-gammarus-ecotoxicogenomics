# EchoGO 0.1.4 interpretation guidance

Functional interpretation is anchored to target-supported GOseq terms and
reviewed functional-family mappings. EchoGO annotation-context results are
reported as hypothesis-generating evidence and are not treated as independent
biological replication or proof of conservation.

The public workflow distinguishes target-supported evidence, matched-background
annotation-context hypotheses, and default-domain exploratory hypotheses. The
S5 candidate-gene panel contains 16 target-supported genes.
